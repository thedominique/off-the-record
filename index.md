# Privacy Policy — Off the Record

**Last updated: 3 September 2026**

Off the Record is a voice-note app for a small circle of people who already
know each other. It has no feed, no recommendations, no advertising and no
strangers. This policy explains what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it.

Off the Record is made and operated by **Dominique Persson**, an individual
developer in Sweden, who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR.
Questions, requests and complaints: **off.the.record.apps@gmail.com**.

## What I store

**Your account.** Your email address, a display name, a handle, an optional
avatar choice (one of a fixed set of pictures that ship inside the app — no
photo is uploaded), and an optional intro sound you record. The email address
identifies your account and is how you reset a password. It is never shown to
other people in the app.

**Your voice notes.** The audio you record and send, along with its length, the
time it was sent, and who it was sent to. Audio is stored so the people you sent
it to can play it.

**Who you are connected to.** Your connections, any pending friend requests,
your groups and their membership, your invite token, and anyone you have
blocked.

**Listen receipts.** Whether you have played a note you received. Senders can
see how many of their recipients have played a note.

**Reports.** If you report a take, I store what you reported, who sent it, the
reason you chose and when. Reports are read by me.

**Push token.** If you allow notifications, a device token so the app can tell
you a tape arrived. Nothing else about your device is collected.

**On your phone only.** Drafts, unsent takes, your recorded intro, cached audio
for tapes on your shelf, and where you stopped in each tape. These live on the
device and are deleted when you discard them or delete the app.

I do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, analytics or
crash telemetry, and there is no third-party tracking in the app.

## What I do not do

I do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it to train anything,
or listen to your voice notes — except the single take named in a report, when
a report has been filed about.

## Who can see what

Nobody can find you unless you let them: profiles are readable only by people
you are connected to, people you share a group with, people with a live friend
request between you, and people you have exchanged a note with. A note can only
be delivered to someone you are connected to, or to a group you are both in.
Blocking someone ends both.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with **Supabase** (Postgres and object storage) and push
notifications are delivered via **Expo**, which passes them to Apple and Google.
These providers process data on my behalf. Audio and database contents are
stored in the region configured for the Supabase project.

## How long it is kept

Notes you have fully listened to are deleted automatically about a week after
you have heard them. A note you delete is removed for everyone who received it.
A tape you clear disappears for you and stays with the others. Reports and their
associated records are kept while the report is open and for a reasonable period
after it is resolved.

Everything else lives until you delete it.

## Deleting your account

**Me → Delete my account**, inside the app. It removes your profile, your
handle, your notes and their audio from every shelf they reached, your
connections, requests, blocks, group memberships and your login. Groups you
created are removed; the tapes other people already received survive, because
they were delivered to them, though they lose the group's name. This is
immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot get into the app to do this, write to
**off.the.record.apps@gmail.com** and I will do it for you.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, and under similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to access,
correct, export or erase your data, to object to processing, and to complain to
a supervisory authority — in Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY),
imy.se.

The app already covers access — everything held about you is visible inside it —
and erasure, above. For anything else, write to
**off.the.record.apps@gmail.com** and I will answer within 30 days.

## Children

Off the Record is not intended for children under 18, and I do not knowingly
create accounts for them. If you believe a child has an account, write to
**off.the.record.apps@gmail.com** and I will remove it.

## Security

Access to every row is enforced by the database itself rather than by the app,
so a modified client cannot read what it is not entitled to. Audio is served
through short-lived signed URLs. No system is perfect; if you find a hole,
please tell me at **off.the.record.apps@gmail.com** before telling anyone else.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the
change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
